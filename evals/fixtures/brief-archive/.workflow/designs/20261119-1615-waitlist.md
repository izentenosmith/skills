# Design: Waitlist for full slots

**Status:** in progress
**Solving:** a guest who finds a slot full has nowhere to go, so the venue loses the booking when a party cancels.
**Why now:** venue groups ask for it at every renewal call.
**Done looks like:** a guest can join a waitlist for a full slot and is offered the slot when it frees up.
**Calibration:** large — a new aggregate, a new entry point and a notification path
**Questions:** ~11 to resolve (policy, data model, interfaces, failure modes, scope, security, back-compat, observability, rollout, testability, decomposition) — 2 resolved

## Decisions
- **Policy vs. details — where the waitlist lives:** a `Waitlist` aggregate beside `Booking`, not a status on it — a waitlisted guest has no slot yet. Rejected: a `waitlisted` flag on `Booking`.
- **Scope — notification channel:** deferred to the notifications team; this design only records who is next.

## Open
- How long a freed slot is held for the next guest (question 3, asked 2026-11-19, no answer yet).
- Whether a waitlisted guest can hold places on several slots at once.
