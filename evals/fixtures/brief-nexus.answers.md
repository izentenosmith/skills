# brief-nexus: answer key (eval 61)

The `cutoff` session's worktree in increment 3 of a booking-rules build, with the nexus's shared notes at `.workflow/nexus/`. The session's log (`sessions/cutoff.md`) opens with the filled-in contract from `skills/development/nexus/contract.md` and ends with the nexus resuming it at agent-brief. Kept outside the fixture folder, because runs copy the folder.

| In the fixture | Expected |
|---|---|
| The design file is `resolved` but its Decisions carry two lines `unresolved` | read both as questions, and read the register before asking either |
| Stored bookings: re-validated when the rule ships? DEC-3 (decided 14 Nov, a day after the design was written, asked by another session) says new rules apply to new submissions only | cite DEC-3 in the brief; no packet |
| The window edge: accepted or rejected? No DEC covers it | a `Q-cutoff-<n>` packet in the log and the reply: options table, recommendation, `Checked:` with the DEC ids read |
| agent-brief's approval gate | a second `Q-cutoff-<n>` packet, "approve this brief"; the brief stays `draft — awaiting approval` |
| The contract's status block | the four lines at the end of the log entry and of the reply, `Waiting on: nexus: Q-…` |
| `decisions.md`, the design file, the other notes, the code | untouched: a session writes its brief and its own log |

The standalone handoff ("Approve it, or tell me what to change") is the wrong ending here: it asks the reader of the reply, and the user is not in this conversation.
