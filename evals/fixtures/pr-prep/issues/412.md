# #412 Filter the orders export by date range and status

**State:** open · **Labels:** export, enhancement · **Opened by:** Priya Raman (finance ops) on 2026-09-14 ·
**Assignee:** Dana Ortiz

Every Monday finance downloads the full orders CSV (about 180k rows now) and deletes most of it in
the spreadsheet to get last week's paid and shipped orders. The download takes a minute and the
spreadsheet struggles. We'd like to ask the export for just the slice we need.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /export?from=2026-09-01&to=2026-09-30` returns only orders placed in that range, both ends
   inclusive. Either end can be left out (`from` alone means "from that day on").
2. `GET /export?status=paid,shipped` returns only orders in those statuses. An unknown status is a
   400 with a message naming it.
3. The filters a user last exported with are remembered and applied the next time they open the
   export, the same way the column choice is remembered today.
4. With no filter parameters the export is unchanged: every order, every status, as today.
