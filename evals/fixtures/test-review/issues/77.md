# LOY-77 · Loyalty points

**Reporter:** Priya Natarajan (Product) · **Assignee:** Ravi Menon · **Status:** In review · **Branch:** `feature/loyalty-points`

## Why

Repeat customers asked for a reason to stay with us instead of the marketplace. Marketing wants a
simple points scheme live before the holiday season, with the Gold tier as the headline.

## What

Customers earn points on every order and spend them as a discount on a later one. Points are kept
in a ledger so finance can audit every change to a balance.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Customers earn 1 point for every whole dollar of the order subtotal. Cents are dropped:
  a $25.99 order earns 25 points.
- **AC-2** A customer whose lifetime earned points are 1,000 or more is **Gold**, and earns 1.5×
  points on each order, rounded down. Lifetime points are the points earned before this order.
- **AC-3** Orders placed in the customer's birthday month earn double points (after the Gold
  multiplier). A customer who hasn't given a birthday month never gets the bonus.
- **AC-4** Points are redeemed in blocks of 100 for $1 off. Redeeming more points than the balance,
  a partial block, or zero or negative points is refused, and the balance is left unchanged.
- **AC-5** Refunding an order takes back the points that order earned. The balance never goes below
  zero.

## Out of scope

- Points expiry (LOY-81).
- Showing the balance in the storefront (LOY-79).
